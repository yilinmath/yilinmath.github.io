Program Outline

Detailed speaker order can be added after confirmation. The structure below follows the five-day workshop format and the talk counts in the application form.

Monday, Jan 18

Opening and foundations

Opening remarks, plenary lectures and focused discussions on Hochschild theory and deformation problems.

Tuesday, Jan 19

Algebra and representation theory

One-hour talks and short communications on derived invariants, support varieties and related algebraic structures.

Wednesday, Jan 20

Geometry and quantization

Sessions on noncommutative geometry, formal methods, deformation quantization and star products.

Thursday, Jan 21

Topology and mirror symmetry

Lectures connecting Hochschild invariants with Fukaya categories, homological mirror symmetry and topology.

Friday, Jan 22

Open problems and collaboration

Final talks, short presentations, problem sessions and concluding discussion.
