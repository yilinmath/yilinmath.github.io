About the Conference

This conference is the seventh of a series of international conferences devoted to Hochschild cohomology with applications in Algebra, Geometry, Topology, and Mathematical Physics. Previous editions have been successfully held in renowned institutes including BIRS (Canada), CIRM (France), Buenos Aires (Argentina), and Oberwolfach (Germany).

Hochschild cohomology, initially developed as a tool for associative algebras, has evolved into a versatile and powerful invariant that threads through diverse branches of modern mathematics. Its core lies in measuring the "noncommutative geometry" of an algebra, probing its deformation theory, smoothness, and hidden structure through a homological lens.

The conference will bring together researchers working on Hochschild cohomology, deformation theory, noncommutative geometry, representation theory, homological mirror symmetry, and algebraic topology to foster interdisciplinary dialogue and collaboration. Emphasis will be placed on recent advances, open problems, and emerging connections to other research fields.
